# VENTRIVA — Compliance Management & Scoring Engine

## Overview
Monitors corporate, legal, financial, tax, and board governance compliance items, calculating real-time venture compliance scores.

## Compliance Metrics
- **Compliance Score**: `(Completed Items / Total Compliance Items) * 100`
- **Automated Overdue Check**: Items past due date with status `Pending` automatically transition to `Overdue` and trigger audit events.
