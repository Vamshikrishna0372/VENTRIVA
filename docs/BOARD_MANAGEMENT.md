# VENTRIVA — Board Operations & Meeting Management

## Overview
The Board Operations module handles director seats, term appointments, meeting scheduling, agenda distribution, quorum checks, and meeting minutes.

## Board Director Roles
- **Founder Director**: Executive director representing founding team.
- **Investor Director**: Non-executive director representing lead venture investors.
- **Independent Director**: External industry expert director.
- **Observer**: Non-voting board meeting participant.
- **Chairperson**: Board leader presiding over meetings.

## Board Meetings & Quorum Verification
- Meeting types: `Regular`, `Special`, `Emergency`, `Annual`, `Committee`.
- Quorum is calculated server-side based on active director attendance.
